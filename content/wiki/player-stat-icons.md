The [Top Players](/top-players) table uses these icons in its column headers, and the same icons appear next to each statistic on a player's page. Hover over (or long-press) a column header to see its full name.

## The icons

<dl class="wiki-icon-key">
<dt>{{> stat-icon name="apps"}}</dt>
<dd><strong>Appearances</strong>: matches the player has played in, as a starter or a substitute.</dd>
<dt>{{> stat-icon name="goals"}}</dt>
<dd><strong>Goals</strong> scored, including penalties.</dd>
<dt>{{> stat-icon name="assists"}}</dt>
<dd><strong>Assists</strong>: passes or touches that directly set up a goal.</dd>
<dt>{{> stat-icon name="ga"}}</dt>
<dd><strong>Goals + assists</strong>: the two added together, a quick measure of direct goal involvement.</dd>
<dt>{{> stat-icon name="rating"}}</dt>
<dd><strong>Average rating</strong>: the mean of the player's match ratings. It uses the same colours as <a href="/wiki/average-team-rating">team ratings</a>.</dd>
<dt>{{> stat-icon name="minutes"}}</dt>
<dd><strong>Minutes</strong> played.</dd>
<dt>{{> stat-icon name="pens"}}</dt>
<dd><strong>Penalties scored</strong>.</dd>
<dt>{{> stat-icon name="penaltiesMissed"}}</dt>
<dd><strong>Penalties missed</strong>: the same icon with a red line through it.</dd>
<dt>{{> stat-icon name="shotsOn"}}</dt>
<dd><strong>Shots on target</strong>: shots that were heading into the goal, whether they were scored or saved.</dd>
<dt><span class="yellow-card" role="img" aria-label="Yellow card"></span></dt>
<dd><strong>Yellow cards</strong>.</dd>
<dt><span class="red-card" role="img" aria-label="Red card"></span></dt>
<dd><strong>Red cards</strong>.</dd>
</dl>

## Which columns you see

Appearances, goals, assists, goals + assists and rating are shown by default. The other columns appear when you switch that statistic on in the table filters, so you can build the view you need without the table becoming too wide to read.

Columns that are easier to read as text, such as non-penalty goals or pass accuracy, use short labels instead of icons. They are all listed in [Abbreviations](/wiki/abbreviations).
